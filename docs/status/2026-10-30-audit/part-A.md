## Server work left

Stage: everything in sections 3–8 is M1 (section 2 table "full main scenario"; section 29 slices S-02, S-03, S-08, S-10), except partners and franchise (M2) and the rider's own route editing (M3). Items marked "before launch" belong to the M1 slices; "can follow launch" means the previous audit (docs/status/2026-10-28-server-audit.md) and the spec allow it to come after the first pilot.

### City and pricing
- **REQ-TEN-05** — a per-city setting that allows or forbids out-of-city drop-offs (and, if needed, a distance limit), checked when the quote is made. M1, before launch (S-04/S-06).

### Rider
- **REQ-RID-05** — in the active order, return a driver card (name, car, colour, plate, and photo once DRV-01 has one) for the current assignment only; hide a REVOKED/RELEASED assignment from the rider snapshot; give a rider-facing reason code for a driver change. M1, before launch (S-07).
- **REQ-RID-06** — a support command to change the destination of an order after the start, with a new price check and audit (M1 "agreed procedure"). M1, before launch (S-08). Rider self-editing is M3.

### Driver
- **REQ-DRV-01** — driver profile photo (upload through `files`, review), admission city on the driver. M1, before launch (S-03). Payout details wait for Q-11.
- **REQ-DRV-07** — order comment / entrance point from rider and operator up to the driver's offer and trip; in the offer, say what kind of sum the price is and the expected earnings after commission; a server time or remaining seconds next to `expires_at`. M1, before launch (S-05/S-07).

### Dispatcher and call centre
- **REQ-OPS-04** (with REQ-DSP-10) — manual offer by the dispatcher to a chosen driver, through the same offer, confirmation, deadline and double-assignment path. M1 per the role table in section 4; the previous audit put it after launch.
- **REQ-OPS-08** — call queues, hold and transfer (dialplan plus server state for the operator), WebRTC transport for an in-browser operator phone. M1 "before the call centre launches" (section 2 table, S-10); the manual order already works without it.

### Admin, support and finance

- **REQ-ADM-03** — correction command for an order's final fare (new final-fare version with a delta, ledger postings, reason, link to the original fact) and for a wrongly recorded driver. M1, before launch (S-08).
- **REQ-ADM-04** — bulk operations: preview of the count, separate permission, background job, result per record, active trips never ended. M1; the previous audit put it after launch.
- **REQ-ADM-10** — ask for the password again on staff rights changes, as for merchants and integrations. M1, before launch (S-02/S-08); small.
